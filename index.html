    About the GaLa model

    The spectrum is modeled as the convolution of a Gaussian of width σ centered at EG with an asymmetric exponential tail function with widths wl (low energy) and wh (high energy):

    • EG (eV): center of the Gaussian, the characteristic emission energy of the ensemble.
    • σ (meV): standard deviation of the Gaussian, describing inhomogeneous broadening.
    • wh (meV): width of the exponential tail on the high-energy side.
    • wl (meV): width of the exponential tail on the low-energy side.
    • y and A: baseline and amplitude of the normalized spectrum (practically fixed by default).

    Wavelength data are converted to energy with E = hc/λ (hc = 1239.841984 eV nm) and the Jacobian IE = Iλ λ²/hc, then normalized to the maximum. GaLx replaces the low-energy Laplace tail with a Lorentzian of half width γ. The fit uses scipy.optimize.curve_fit; uncertainties are 1σ from the covariance matrix.

    Model code: github.com/leoluber/gala (MIT license) · Preprint: doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-8603272/v1 · Paper: Nano Lett. 2026
